THE NAME

The great houses are named for their founders.
This one is named for the forces that founded luxury.

Valeur

FR. · NOUN · VA-LUR

i. Worth; what a thing is valued at.
ii. In French finance, a share — a stock traded on the market.

&

Désir

FR. · NOUN · DAY-ZEER

i. Want; longing.
ii. The force that makes a thing precious long before it is priced.

LVMH
